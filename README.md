# Simply Green Business Card

A simple digital business card created as another piece of Simply Green and Garden Spray’s small digital presence.

This project was built to support the business owner during in-person customer interactions. Instead of relying only on a traditional paper business card, the owner can open the page on their phone and show customers a QR code. The QR code sends customers directly to the Simply Green website, where they can view services and contact information.

The goal was to make the business easier to share without changing how the company operates.

## Overview

The Simply Green Business Card is a small web page that hosts a PDF version of the business card.

It acts as a phone-friendly handoff tool. The owner can show the QR code in person, and customers can scan it to reach the main website. This keeps the business card useful while connecting it to the company’s digital presence.

## Features

* Simple hosted business card page
* PDF-based card display
* QR code linking to the Simply Green website
* Phone-friendly layout
* Easy customer handoff during in-person visits
* Supports traditional word-of-mouth workflow
* Extends the Simply Green digital presence
* Lightweight static website structure
* Built for practical use by the business owner

## What I Learned

* How to connect print-style business materials to a website
* How QR codes support in-person customer interactions
* How to design a small digital tool for a non-digital workflow
* How to make a business card useful beyond paper
* How to keep a digital asset simple and easy to access
* How small web pages can support a larger customer experience
